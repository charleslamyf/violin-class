The 10-week class

This is what we learn in 10 weeks

Every skill builds toward one goal: playing a simple song together at the end of the term.

  1. 1

    Violin & bow hold

    Holding the violin and bow comfortably and correctly.

  2. 2

    Basic posture

    Standing and sitting tall, relaxed shoulders, good habits from day one.

  3. 3

    String plucking

    Pizzicato on each string to learn the instrument before bowing.

  4. 4

    Open strings

    Bowing the open G, D, A and E strings with a clear, steady sound.

  5. 5

    Basic note playing

    Placing the first fingers to play new notes on the strings.

  6. 6

    A simple song

    Putting it all together to learn a complete tune.

  7. 7

    Ensemble skills

    Starting together, keeping the beat and listening as a group.

After 10 weeks

Your child can perform a simple song on the violin, on stage with their classmates.
